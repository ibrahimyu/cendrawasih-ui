# Example

A calm teal and sun palette that ships with the system for two reasons: to show that every component works outside Cendra, and to copy when starting a new brand (see [Adding a brand](../adding-a-brand.md)). It is not a real brand; don't ship a product on it.

Pick it in the playground's Brand menu, or open the playground with `?brand=example`.

- Five roles: teal `primary`, coral `secondary`, sky `tertiary` (with navy text), sun `spotlight` (with teal text), green `accent`.
- No font files: both faces fall back to the system sans.
- A simple sun as its mark, in `tokens/brands/example/mark.svg`.
- Teal lifts to #4d93a6 for outlines on Night; primary and spotlight get their own tints.
