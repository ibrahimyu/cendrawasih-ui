## What you may do

- Use Cendrawasih UI, including the name *Cendrawasih UI*, to build your own products, and say that they are built with it.
- Read and learn from the Cendra brand files as an example of how a brand plugs into the system.
- Use the Cendra brand stylesheet and assets in work done for or with Cendra.

## What you may not do

- Ship your own product with the Cendra brand (`brands/cendra.css`, the logos, the mark or the illustrations), or in a way that suggests it is made or endorsed by Cendra.
- Use the Cendra name or mark as part of your product's name, logo or domain.

To build on the system with your own look, start from the Example brand and follow `docs/adding-a-brand.md`. The palette roles, components and everything else stay yours to use under the MIT license.
